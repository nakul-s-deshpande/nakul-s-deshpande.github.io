Research

Creep

How soil moves downhill on slopes too gentle to slide. In a laboratory hillslope, watched with lasers, it creeps even when nothing disturbs it.

Acoustics

Thin films and small sensors, played with sound and read with a laser, to work out how stiff they are and how tightly they're held.

Logjams

Two months in the Idaho wilderness watching a logjam that, it turned out, wasn't jammed.

About

I study the physics of slowly deforming landscapes: how soil and rock creep, flow, and fail, and what that does to the shape of the land over time. I work in the field, in the lab, and on the computer.

I'm a postdoc at Los Alamos National Laboratory. Before that I did my Ph.D. in Earth & Environmental Science at the University of Pennsylvania.
